# Updating Your Website — The Easy Way
### A one-page guide for Courtney · Wander & Roam Travel

**The most important thing first: you cannot break your live website by asking for a change.**
Nothing goes public until you've seen it and approved it. Every request just creates a
private draft for you to look at.

---

## How it works — 3 steps

1. **Say what you want.** Open Claude (the website version Fabrizio will set up for you)
   and describe the change in your own words. No special language needed.
2. **Look at the preview.** You'll get a link to a private draft of your site with the
   change in it. Open it on your phone, just like a normal webpage, and see how it looks.
   Nobody else can see this draft.
3. **Approve it, or don't.** Happy with it? Tap **Merge** — that's the publish button,
   and your live site updates within a minute or so. Not happy? Just reply to Claude
   with what to fix ("make the photo bigger," "use their full names"), or close the
   draft and forget it. Your live site never changed.

**Changed your mind after publishing?** No problem. Everything is saved, and any
change can be undone. Ask Claude — or Fabrizio — to "undo the last change."

---

## Things you can just ask for (copy these and fill in the blanks)

- *"Fix a typo: on the About section, 'itineray' should be 'itinerary.'"*
- *"Add this testimonial to the Travelers section: '[paste the client's words]' — from [name]."*
- *"Swap the big photo at the top of the homepage for the one attached."*
- *"Add a new specialty card called '[name]' that says: [your description]."*
- *"Update my specialties: change '[old wording]' to '[new wording].'"*
- *"Add a note at the top of the page that says I'm now booking holiday travel for December."*

**Tips that make it work first try:**
- Say **which part of the page** you mean — "the Travelers section," "the top photo,"
  "near the contact form." (The big photo at the very top is called the *hero* photo,
  if you ever hear that word.)
- For testimonials, paste the client's **exact words** — Claude won't rewrite them.
- If a photo is involved, **attach it** to your message.

## If something looks wrong

- **In the preview:** don't approve it. Tell Claude what looks off, in plain words.
- **On the live site:** text Fabrizio, or ask Claude to undo it. There's always an
  "undo" — a change is never permanent.
